A small circle, just for your people.

cocoon is an Android app for sharing photos, short videos and music clips with friends you approve. What you share is encrypted on your phone before it leaves it.

Private by design. Posts are end-to-end encrypted. Only the friends you approve can open them.

Sign in with Google. Used only to know who you are. cocoon never reads your Gmail, Contacts or your own Drive.

Music with your post. Pick the part of a song, up to 30 seconds, that plays with your photo or video.
