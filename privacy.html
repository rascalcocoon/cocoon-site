Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

cocoon ("the app", "we") is an Android app for sharing photos, videos and music clips with friends you approve. This page explains what it collects and what happens to it.

What we receive

  • From your Google account, when you sign in: your name, email address and an account identifier. We use the Sign in with Google ID token only to know who you are. We do not request access to your Gmail, Contacts, Calendar or your own Google Drive.
  • What you post: photos, videos, captions, comments, likes, music clips, your profile name and avatar, and your friend list.
  • Technical data: a public encryption key made on your phone, and time stamps of files.

How your posts are protected

Posts, comments, likes, music clips and your friend list are encrypted on your phone before they are uploaded. The keys that open them stay on your device (and in the recovery key, if you create one). Only the friends you approve receive the means to open a post. We cannot read them.

Photo location data is removed from pictures before they are shared.

Where it is stored

Encrypted files are stored in a Google Drive account that belongs to the cocoon operator, reached through a Google Apps Script service. This means Google processes the data as our hosting provider. The person running cocoon can see that encrypted files exist, their names, sizes and time stamps, but not what they contain.

What other people can see

  • To let friends find you, a registry entry with your display name, email address, public key and small avatar is visible to other cocoon users, who can search it.
  • Your public profile (name, avatar) and your encrypted posts are readable by cocoon users, but only friends you approved can decrypt the posts.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not share your data with third parties, except Google as our hosting provider, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

  • You can delete your posts in the app.
  • You can sign out; this removes your sign-in from the phone.
  • To have your registry entry and stored files removed completely, or to ask about your data, email rascalcocoon@gmail.com. We will act on it within 30 days.
  • You can also remove cocoon's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

cocoon is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.
